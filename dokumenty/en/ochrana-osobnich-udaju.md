## 4. Your rights

You have the right:
- to **access** your data and to information about how it is processed,
- to have inaccurate data **rectified**,
- to have your data **erased** if it is no longer needed or if its processing is not lawful,
- to **restrict** processing,
- to **data portability** for data we process on the basis of a contract,
- to **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest,
- to **withdraw your consent** at any time where processing is based on consent (e.g. analytics cookies). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Please send your request to %EMAIL%. We will handle it without undue delay and no later than within one month.

If you believe that we are processing your data unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the **Office for Personal Data Protection** (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, Czech Republic, [www.uoou.gov.cz](https://www.uoou.gov.cz), or with the supervisory authority in the EU member state where you live.
