# Withdrawal from the Contract

If you are a consumer, you may withdraw from a purchase contract concluded at a distance **without giving any reason within 14 days of receiving the goods**. The detailed rules, including exceptions (e.g. jewelry made to order based on your specifications), can be found in Article 5 of the [Terms and Conditions](#dokument-obchodni-podminky).

## How to withdraw

You may choose any of the following methods:
- using the [Withdraw from the contract](#odstoupit) button directly on the website – this is the fastest way, and we will send you a confirmation of receipt by email,
- by email to %EMAIL%,
- by letter to the address %ADRESA_VRACENI%,
- by completing the model form below and sending it by email or by post.

## What happens next

1. Please send the jewelry back to us no later than 14 days after withdrawing, to the address %ADRESA_VRACENI%. You bear the cost of returning the goods. We do not accept cash-on-delivery shipments. We recommend sending the jewelry back complete (including the box and the certificate) as an insured shipment.
2. We will refund your money within 14 days of the withdrawal, using the same means of payment you used. We are not obliged to refund the money before we receive the jewelry or before you prove that it has been sent back.
3. If the jewelry shows signs of wear or damage, we may claim compensation for its diminished value.
