# Cookie Policy

Effective from October 13, 2026

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Your browser’s local storage (localStorage) works in a similar way.

## Which cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** The website does not need any cookies to function. In your browser’s local storage we only save your cookie preference and your chosen language, so that we do not have to ask you on every visit. We do not need your consent to store these settings.

| Name | Purpose | Type | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| cookie-souhlas | stores your cookie preference | browser local storage | 12 months |
| jazyk | stores your chosen website language | browser local storage | until deleted in the browser |

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic. Its cookies are only enabled **with your explicit consent**, which you can give in the banner when you first visit the website. Without your consent, they are not stored. We do not use marketing cookies.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| _ga | Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics) | distinguishing visitors for traffic statistics | 2 years |
| _ga_* | Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics) | keeping the session state for traffic statistics | 2 years |

## Third-party services

When the page loads, a technical library used to display the catalog is downloaded from the Cloudflare network (cdnjs). Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser information but does not store any cookies. Forms are sent via the Web3Forms service, which does not store any cookies either. When you start filling in a form, the hCaptcha service (Intuition Machines, Inc.) is loaded to verify that the form is not being submitted by a robot. It processes technical data about your device and may use your browser’s storage to do so. This is a necessary protection of the forms, so we do not need your consent for it.

## How to change your consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the website footer. You can also delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings.

More information about the processing of personal data can be found in our [Privacy Policy](#dokument-ochrana-osobnich-udaju).
