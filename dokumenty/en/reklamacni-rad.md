## 1. What the Seller is liable for

1.1. The Seller is liable to the Buyer for the goods being free from defects upon receipt, in particular for the goods matching the agreed description, type, quantity and quality, being fit for the purpose for which such goods are normally used, and being delivered with the agreed accessories (a box and a certificate).

If a defect becomes apparent within one year of receipt, the goods are deemed to have been defective upon receipt, unless this is precluded by the nature of the goods or of the defect.

## 2. What is not a defect

The following, in particular, are not defects of the goods:
- natural characteristics of natural stones – inclusions, shades of color, differences in size and shape compared with the photograph to the extent described in the Terms and Conditions,
- normal wear and tear corresponding to the length and manner of use – abrasions, scratches on the metal or the stone, loss of shine,
- natural tarnishing (oxidation) of silver,
- gradual wear of the surface finish (rhodium or gold plating) through use,
- damage caused by impact, a fall, pressure or snagging – e.g. a chipped or cracked stone, a deformed ring, a bent or broken clasp, or a stone falling out as a result of mechanical damage to its setting,
- damage caused by chemicals (cleaning agents, chlorine, cosmetics, perfumes) or by improper care contrary to the instructions in the Terms and Conditions,
- damage caused by unprofessional repair, resizing or any other intervention by a third party,
- a defect that the Buyer was made aware of before concluding the contract and for which a lower price was agreed.

## 3. How to make a complaint

3.1. Please notify the Seller of the defect without undue delay after discovering it, by email to %EMAIL% or by telephone at %TELEFON%. Please state:
- your name and contact details,
- the identification of the piece (code) and the date of purchase, ideally with proof of purchase,
- a description of the defect and photographs,
- the remedy you are requesting (see Article 4).

3.2. Please send the piece to the address %ADRESA_VRACENI% or, by appointment, hand it over in person. The Seller does not accept cash-on-delivery shipments. We recommend packing the piece carefully and sending it as an insured shipment.

3.3. The Seller will issue a written (email) confirmation to the Buyer stating when the complaint was received, what it concerns and which remedy the Buyer is requesting.

3.4. The Buyer is entitled to reimbursement of the reasonable costs incurred in connection with a justified complaint (e.g. postage).

## 4. Consumer rights

4.1. If the goods are defective, the consumer may request that the defect be remedied by **repair** or by **replacement**. The Seller may refuse the requested remedy if it is impossible or disproportionately costly compared with the other one. Since jewelry with natural stones consists of unique pieces, replacement is often impossible and the defect is remedied by repair.

4.2. The Seller may refuse to remedy the defect if doing so is impossible or disproportionately costly.

4.3. The consumer may request a **reasonable price reduction** or **withdraw from the contract** if:
- the Seller has refused to remedy the defect or has failed to remedy it in accordance with the law,
- the defect reappears,
- the defect constitutes a material breach of the contract, or
- it is clear from the Seller’s statement or from the circumstances that the defect will not be remedied within a reasonable time or without significant inconvenience to the consumer.

4.4. If the defect is insignificant, the consumer may not withdraw from the contract but may request a price reduction.

## 5. Handling the complaint

5.1. The Seller will handle the complaint and remedy the defect without undue delay, **no later than 30 days** after the complaint was made, unless a longer period is agreed with the consumer.

5.2. Once the complaint has been handled, the Seller will issue a confirmation to the Buyer stating the date and the manner in which it was handled and, where applicable, the duration of the repair, or a written justification of the rejection of the complaint.

5.3. If the complaint is rejected, the Buyer may have the defect assessed by an independent expert. If, on the basis of the expert opinion, the Seller acknowledges that the complaint is justified, the Seller will reimburse the Buyer for the cost of the expert opinion.

5.4. After the complaint has been handled, the Seller will ask the Buyer to collect the piece. The complaint is concluded upon collection of the piece.

## 6. Buyers who are entrepreneurs

Sections 2099 et seq. of the Czech Civil Code and Article 10 of the Terms and Conditions apply to Buyers who are entrepreneurs. Paragraphs 1.2 and 3.4 and Article 4 of this Complaints Procedure do not apply to them; the Seller decides on the manner in which the defect will be remedied.

## 7. Out-of-court dispute resolution

If no agreement is reached, the consumer may seek out-of-court resolution of the dispute through the **Czech Trade Inspection Authority** ([adr.coi.cz](https://adr.coi.cz)); details are set out in Article 8 of the [Terms and Conditions](#dokument-obchodni-podminky).
